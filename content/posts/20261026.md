You Can See Everything employs a highly innovative narrative expression, one that thoroughly blurs the boundary between life and image, as well as the boundary between truth and falsehood.

What is interesting is that this choice is not made to be deliberately mysterious. It is a necessary approach the creator had to adopt in order to express the truth clearly.

The more we ponder what is true and what is false, the more blurred the line between true and false becomes.
