Ask an AI coding tool to make a game and the first thing it reaches for is a landing page. A headline, three feature cards, a start button floating in the middle of a gradient. Ask it for an interface and you get a monitoring dashboard instead, dark panels and small numbers ticking upward. A game is neither of those, but they are the two shapes the model has seen a million times, and the average of the web is marketing.

The first thing I do now is take both shapes off the table. I point the model at one specific game on Steam and tell it to study how that game opens. Then I say out loud that the thing being built is a place and not a page. The output changes as soon as it has real grammar to copy. The camera sits at the height a person would stand at. A foreground moves faster than the background. The player is small, and the world was not arranged around them.

If you have no method of your own yet, three constraints will carry you most of the way.

Cut the text until it feels too little, then cut again. Put every word on the same level. No headings, no subtitles, no caption explaining what the player is supposed to feel. A game that narrates itself has already become a website.

Forbid drawing with code. Canvas, DOM elements, SVG paths, each one is a route back to the browser. The model should call an image generation tool, or a 3D MCP, and place whatever comes back.

Then ask for more layers of image assets than the scene seems to need. Foreground, midground, background, particles, light, vignette, something blurred far behind. Depth is the feeling. One generated background is wallpaper. Three layers of the same scene is a place.

None of this makes a game good. It only clears the room so a game can happen.

Five small games came out of this, [Oracle](https://noahisarider.itch.io/oracle), [The Little Hectic](https://noahisarider.itch.io/the-little-hectic), [ICONS](https://noahisarider.itch.io/icons), [PixelCowboy](https://noahisarider.itch.io/pixelcowboy) and [Sisyphus](https://noahisarider.itch.io/sisyphus), all on [my itch.io page](https://noahisarider.itch.io/).
