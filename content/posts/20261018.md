When developing a game with AI coding tools, you have to require the AI to abandon its landing page thinking and its monitoring dashboard design thinking. It is best to make it find a standard game on Steam as a reference. Only then can it produce a game with a sense of immersion, instead of a web project.

If you have not yet formed your own experience, you can first try asking the AI to do the following. Reduce the text as much as possible, and the text cannot have any hierarchy. Do not allow any drawing with code, it must call an image generation tool or another drawing or modeling MCP. The more image assets the better, and they absolutely must be layered.
