Blood, severed limbs, body fluids and excrement are obscene and disgusting things, because they symbolize the boundary of the human body.

Cult slasher films take the obscene matter of these boundaries and make it into a DJ's mixtape, played at maximum volume at a party, and this symbolizes the disappearance of boundaries.

And the phrase “the disappearance of boundaries” can necessarily fuse with a queer spirit, so queer themes and slasher themes are naturally connected.

In the film Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma, the female lead enjoys the sexual pleasure of being a lesbian at the moment the killer completes the massacre, and this is one of the typical cases of that theory.
