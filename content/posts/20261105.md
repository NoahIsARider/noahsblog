Just as Sinners places Black people's love of pleasure at the center of the stage, critiques the hypocritical white gaze, and ultimately achieves self-recognition, films like Jim Queen and Lesbian Space Princess likewise reflect back every gaze through exaggerated self-display. In these films, it is the audience that is being gazed at, not these minority groups. They cannot be gazed at precisely because they expose themselves entirely to the gaze of others.
