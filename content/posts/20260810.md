I think countless AI-Human collaboration frameworks operate via a very similar mechanism.

Independent Variable: The framework;

Dependent Variable: Performance;

Mediator Variables: Human perception, fatigue, technical debt, etc.

Moderator Variables: Organizational meta-knowledge, including both AI capability and human capability.

So, if you’re evaluating a framework’s utility or designing one from scratch, you really ought to position yourself within this mechanistic framework.
